## Logline

North Sea, around 1400. Klaus Störtebeker, captain of the Likedeeler — the "equal sharers" — raids the rich ships of the Hanseatic League and splits every take alike among his crew: a criminal to the merchants, a hero to the poor. Captured and condemned in Hamburg, he makes one last bargain — and legend says that after the axe fell, he walked headless past eleven of his men to win their freedom.

## The legend hook

He robbed the richest trading league on earth — and shared the loot equally. A Robin Hood of the North Sea: swashbuckling spectacle, a crew bound by one rule (*what we take, we share alike*), and an ending no audience forgets.

## The teaser

Three shots, one promise:

1. **The storm** — a Hanseatic cog rides heavy northern seas at dusk.
2. **The share** — on deck, in wind and rain, the captain watches gold poured into a common pile and divided equally among the crew.
3. **The walk** — dawn in the harbour; the captain walks tall past a line of kneeling men, fading to black.
